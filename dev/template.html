What runs here

      
    
        
  • The addon's Lua files, embedded unchanged: sh_config, sh_presets, sh_drivetrain, cl_synth, cl_soundbank and cl_engine. They run in a real Lua VM (Lua 5.4, compiled to WebAssembly) in a background thread.
    • 
        
  • The sounds are generated sample by sample through sound.Generate, exactly as each client does in game. It takes longer here (about 15–20 s per engine, against 2 s in game), because the browser's Lua has no JIT compiler.
    • 
        
  • The game's sound calls are replayed in Web Audio the way Source handles them. Volume and pitch change once per frame. Pitch is rounded down to a whole percent, volume is capped at 100%, and the output has no limiter, so anything that would clip in game clips here. Watch the Clipped counter.
    • 
      

    

    

      
What differs from the game

      
    
        
  • Driving is a simple stand-in for Source's vehicle physics: power, grip, braking and the script's top speed. The revs and gears still come from the addon's own gearbox model.
    • 
        
  • Distance falloff uses Source's sound-level formula (85 dB). Roadside, you stand 8 m from a straight road and the car comes round every 500 m. Source's room effects aren't modelled.
    • 
        
  • One car only, and no server. The page plays the server's part: it switches the engine on and off, networks the keys and applies a tuned profile.

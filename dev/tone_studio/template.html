Firing rhythm · one 720° cycle

Each bar is one cylinder firing, coloured by exhaust side (left / right). Uneven bars are what makes a burble or a growl; the two rhythm sliders shape them.

Custom pattern: drag a bar sideways to move that firing, up or down to change its strength, and click it to swap its exhaust side.
